# Migra Website Strategy and Product Direction

**Purpose:** Current website audit and proposed direction for a trusted migration platform serving Nigeria and West Africa  
**Audience:** Founder, product, design, engineering, operations, and growth teams  
**Status:** Working strategic direction  
**Date:** 15 October 2026

## Strategic conclusion

Migra should evolve from a marketing website that routes most users to WhatsApp into a trusted migration operating system. The new experience should help a person understand where they can realistically go, why a pathway fits, what could block them, what it will cost, what to do next, and when expert help is required.

WhatsApp should remain an important access channel, especially in Nigeria and West Africa. It should no longer carry the full burden of discovery, explanation, qualification, trust, and case management. The website should make Migra's intelligence visible, useful, and credible before a user speaks to anyone.

This document covers:

- What the current website communicates well and where it creates uncertainty
- The recommended product and brand position
- Priority audiences, journeys, information architecture, and page responsibilities
- A phased feature roadmap separating the launch website from the larger platform vision
- Trust, evidence, content governance, and measurement requirements

This direction should be refined after reviewing the founder conversation and before visual design begins.

## Executive summary

### The opportunity

Migration from West Africa is not mainly an information shortage. It is a decision and trust problem. People face scattered government requirements, changing rules, unverified agents, unrealistic promises, unclear costs, and no dependable way to understand how their profile affects their options.

Migra can organize that complexity into a structured journey.

### What Migra appears to be today

The live website positions Migra as an AI-powered migration platform combining:

- Pathway discovery through WhatsApp
- Human migration specialists
- Work, study, family, and relocation pathways
- Application and document support
- Client accounts and progress tracking
- A vetted partner-agent network
- Free discovery followed by paid professional support

The underlying model is compelling: trusted information, intelligent matching, and verified human execution.

### The central gap

The brand promises intelligence, clarity, and an end-to-end platform, but most visible journeys end in one generic WhatsApp conversation. Pathway pages provide only high-level descriptions. Users cannot yet see a structured assessment, explainable match, personalized roadmap, complete cost model, or evidence trail.

The website therefore asks users to trust the promise before experiencing the product.

### Recommended direction

Build the new website around a free personalized assessment that produces an explainable pathway shortlist and readiness roadmap. Let people explore evidence-backed country and pathway information without an account. Introduce registration when saving results, managing documents, or working with a specialist becomes valuable.

| From | To |
|---|---|
| Generic WhatsApp entry point | Guided assessment with a personalized result |
| Destination browsing | Profile-led pathway discovery and comparison |
| Broad platform claims | Visible tools, evidence, and operating proof |
| Agent trust by assertion | Transparent verification and accountability |
| One-off consultation | Persistent migration plan and case workspace |
| Content pages | Maintained migration intelligence with source dates |

## Current website audit

The review covered the public homepage, services, migration pathways, resources, client registration, and partner-agent application experience on [Migra.ng](https://migra.ng).

### What already works

- **Strong positioning:** “Migration, without the confusion” is clear, memorable, and connected to the user's anxiety.
- **Accurate problem framing:** The site identifies conflicting information, unreliable agents, hidden costs, and uncertainty.
- **Appropriate access channel:** WhatsApp-first discovery fits established behavior in Nigeria and lowers the barrier to starting.
- **Technology plus people:** The product recognizes that AI alone is insufficient for a high-stakes, regulated journey.
- **Understandable service ladder:** Discover, Navigate, and Relocate create a natural progression.
- **Marketplace foundation:** The partner-agent application introduces a supply-side model in which professionals are vetted before receiving cases.
- **Modern identity:** The design is optimistic, mobile-friendly, and recognizably relevant without feeling stereotypical.
- **Honest language:** “No guaranteed visas” and “no false promises” can become a powerful trust position.

### Where the experience underdelivers

| Observed issue | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Most calls to action lead to the same WhatsApp message | Users cannot experience meaningful differences between discovery, assessment, planning, and specialist support. |
| Pathway pages are short and non-specific | The product promise of clarity is not demonstrated before lead capture. |
| No visible personalized assessment | AI-powered matching remains a claim rather than a useful experience. |
| Little comparison support | Users cannot weigh countries, routes, cost, time, requirements, and trade-offs. |
| Limited proof of source quality | High-stakes guidance requires official sources, update dates, and clear ownership. |
| Trust claims lack visible substantiation | Large success, reach, and security claims may reduce credibility if evidence is absent. |

## Trust and consistency risks

| Current signal | Risk | Recommended treatment |
|---|---|---|
| “No account to create” alongside prominent registration | The journey feels contradictory. | Allow anonymous exploration and explain when an account becomes useful. |
| Migra, Migra.ng, and MigraTech used interchangeably | Brand recognition and legal accountability become unclear. | Define one customer-facing brand, one company identity, and naming rules. |
| 25,000 community figure and 10,000 assisted figure | Visitors may interpret different metrics as conflicting. | Label each metric precisely and define its period and methodology. |
| 50-plus countries versus a small visible pathway set | Breadth may appear overstated. | Separate information coverage from actively supported routes. |
| 98 percent success rate | The claim invites scrutiny and possible regulatory risk. | Publish the cohort, period, denominator, outcome definition, and exclusions—or remove it. |
| “World-class security” and encrypted storage | Security language requires operational proof. | Describe actual controls, access, retention, deletion, and incident processes. |
| Certified experts and vetted agents | Users cannot inspect credentials or verification status. | Show verification criteria, credentials, jurisdictions, status, and complaints processes. |

### Product maturity assessment

The strongest visible capability today is a service-led funnel supported by WhatsApp and human operations. The website hints at a broader platform involving accounts, documents, payments, dashboards, case matching, and partner earnings.

The next website should represent what is operationally true at launch while creating a coherent path toward the larger system.

## Strategic product thesis

### The position to own

Migra should become the trusted migration operating system for Africans: one place to discover realistic pathways, understand eligibility, build readiness, work with verified experts, manage an application, and prepare for life after approval.

### The initial wedge

The strongest entry product is a free, profile-led migration assessment. It should create immediate value without claiming to determine legal eligibility or guarantee an outcome.

| Step | User receives | Migra gains |
|---|---|---|
| Profile | A short assessment covering goals, education, work, language, budget, and family context | Structured demand data |
| Match | A ranked shortlist of plausible pathways | Explainable matching opportunities |
| Explain | Reasons for each match, constraints, and missing information | Trust through transparency |
| Plan | A readiness score and sequenced action plan | A persistent user relationship |
| Review | Optional human verification of the profile and plan | Qualified service conversion |
| Execute | Application, document, and relocation support | Revenue and outcome data |

### What the product must never imply

- A match is the same as legal eligibility.
- A score predicts visa approval without a defensible model.
- Migra replaces official government guidance or regulated legal advice.
- Payment guarantees a visa, admission, employment, or settlement outcome.
- Every listed country or route is actively serviced by Migra.

## Priority audiences and jobs

| Audience | Primary job | What they need to trust |
|---|---|---|
| Explorers | Understand whether migration is realistic and where to begin | Advice is honest, relevant, and free from pressure |
| Destination-decided users | Validate a chosen country or pathway | Requirements are current and sources are official |
| Application-ready users | Prepare documents and submit correctly | The specialist is competent and the process is accountable |
| Blocked applicants | Understand weaknesses and alternatives | Migra will say no when a route is unrealistic |
| Families | Coordinate dependants, cost, timing, and settlement | The plan covers the household |
| Students and early-career users | Compare schools, scholarships, and post-study options | Total cost and employability are not hidden |
| Verified specialists | Receive suitable cases and manage work | Matching, payment, scope, and expectations are controlled |
| Employers and institutions | Reach qualified, prepared candidates | Profiles and partners are verified |

### Design principles

1. **Profile before destination.** Start with the person, not a wall of flags.
2. **Explain every recommendation.** Show the conditions, evidence, and uncertainty behind it.
3. **Use progressive disclosure.** Ask only what is required for the next useful answer.
4. **Place human help at meaningful moments.** Escalate when judgment, regulation, or reassurance matters.
5. **Design mobile-first and bandwidth-aware.** The core journey must work clearly on a modest phone and connection.
6. **Represent local reality by default.** Use naira, local documents, West African qualifications, and practical constraints.
7. **Treat trust as interface, not copy.** Show sources, dates, people, scope, status, and recourse.

## Recommended website architecture

The website should explain the company, deliver useful migration intelligence, and move qualified users into a persistent product journey.

| Primary area | Purpose | Key pages or modules |
|---|---|---|
| Start | Turn uncertainty into a next step | Homepage, assessment entry, goal selection |
| Discover | Explore and compare realistic options | Pathway finder, countries, pathway details, comparison |
| Prepare | Build readiness and reduce mistakes | Roadmap, document checklist, cost planner, language and credential guidance |
| Opportunities | Connect pathways with outcomes | Verified jobs, schools, scholarships, employers, and events |
| Get help | Introduce the right professional support | Plans, specialist matching, consultation, and application support |
| Trust | Make evidence and accountability visible | Verification, experts, outcomes, security, complaints, and policies |
| Workspace | Maintain the user's journey | Saved matches, tasks, documents, messages, payments, and timeline |
| Partners | Build and govern the supply side | Standards, application, portal, and case workflow |
| Learn | Capture demand and educate users | Guides, explainers, glossary, updates, and scam alerts |

### Recommended top navigation

`Explore pathways` · `Countries` · `Opportunities` · `How Migra works` · `Pricing` · `Resources`

- Primary action: **Check my options**
- Secondary action: **Sign in**
- Persistent support option: **WhatsApp**

WhatsApp should remain available as support, not the only product entry.

## Homepage plan

The homepage should move visitors from recognition to useful action. It should not explain every service before the visitor identifies their goal.

| Section | Responsibility | Recommended content |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | State the outcome and start the product | Direct promise, short explanation, and “Check my options” |
| Goal selector | Make the experience personal | Work, study, join family, start a business, relocate, or “I am not sure” |
| How it works | Reduce ambiguity | Profile, matches, plan, expert review, and managed support |
| Example result | Demonstrate intelligence | Realistic sample match with reasons, constraints, sources, and next steps |
| Supported pathways | Set honest scope | Active destinations and routes separated from information-only coverage |
| Trust system | Explain why Migra should be believed | Verification, specialists, official sources, security, and outcome definitions |
| Plans | Explain when payment begins | Free discovery, professional review, and managed support |
| Stories | Show lived outcomes | Starting profile, challenges, support provided, and result |
| Final action | Offer the right next step | Start assessment, continue in WhatsApp, or speak with a specialist |

### Hero direction

Lead with clarity and agency rather than escape or aspiration alone. Avoid leading with a large success statistic, destination collage, or generic consultation button.

Working message direction:

> Find the migration pathways that fit your profile. Answer a few questions to understand your realistic options, requirements, costs, and next steps.

The wording should be refined after reviewing the founder conversation.

## Core user journeys

### I do not know where to start

Homepage → goal selection → progressive assessment → ranked shortlist → comparison → roadmap → save results or request human review.

### I already have a destination

Country or pathway search → requirements and fit check → personal gap analysis → checklist → specialist review or self-guided preparation.

### I am ready to apply

Readiness audit → scope and pricing → verified specialist assignment → document workspace → milestones, messages, payments, and decision.

### I need an opportunity

Profile and goal → verified jobs, programmes, or scholarships → eligibility filters → preparation requirements → external or supported application.

Opportunity listings must never blur employment, admission, and visa eligibility.

### I am a migration specialist

Standards and value proposition → application → identity and credential verification → scope approval → partner portal → matched cases, communication, milestones, payments, and performance review.

### Assessment experience requirements

- Show the estimated time and why sensitive questions are needed.
- Allow uncertainty and incomplete answers instead of forcing false precision.
- Save progress locally before requiring registration where appropriate.
- Explain preliminary matching, professional assessment, and legal eligibility.
- Return value when no strong pathway is found: blockers, preparation steps, and alternatives.
- Offer WhatsApp continuation with consent and a clear summary of transferred information.

## Feature priorities

### P0 launch essentials

| Feature | Launch definition |
|---|---|
| Guided profile assessment | Short branching questionnaire covering goals and major eligibility factors |
| Explainable pathway matches | Ranked options with fit reasons, constraints, confidence, and next steps |
| Structured pathway pages | Requirements, costs, timing, official sources, update date, and support status |
| Comparison | Compare a small number of routes using consistent criteria |
| Trust centre | Verification, specialists, evidence policy, outcomes, security, and complaints |
| Conversion and handoff | Save results, request review, or continue through WhatsApp with context |

### P1 journey workspace

| Feature | Definition |
|---|---|
| Personal roadmap | Sequenced tasks based on the pathway and profile gaps |
| Document readiness | Checklist, status, secure upload, and review notes |
| Cost planner | Government, professional, education, travel, proof-of-funds, and settlement categories |
| Client workspace | Matches, tasks, documents, payments, messages, and timeline |
| Verified specialist profiles | Jurisdiction, credentials, experience, languages, status, and service scope |

### P2 network platform

| Feature | Definition |
|---|---|
| Opportunity marketplace | Verified jobs, schools, scholarships, and employers linked to pathways |
| Settlement services | Pre-departure and destination support through verified providers |
| Partner operating system | Case assignment, service levels, audit trail, payouts, and quality controls |

The launch should not attempt to automate every migration decision. A smaller, well-sourced set of pathways with strong explanations will create more trust than a large catalogue with shallow or stale information.

## Trust architecture

Trust must be designed into data, people, process, and language.

| Layer | What users should see |
|---|---|
| Information | Official sources, last-reviewed date, change history, jurisdiction, and content owner |
| Recommendations | Why the pathway appeared, influential answers, uncertainty, and review requirements |
| Specialists | Identity status, credentials, jurisdiction, permitted scope, languages, experience, and standing |
| Opportunities | Verification type, provider identity, listing date, expiry, and fraud-reporting route |
| Commercial | Scope, inclusions, exclusions, refund terms, government fees, and third-party costs |
| Case work | Named owner, milestones, timestamps, decisions, document access log, and escalation route |
| Security | Data purpose, encryption, access rules, retention, deletion, and incident contact |
| Outcomes | Metric definition, cohort, period, denominator, and limitations |

### Content governance

- Every pathway must have an accountable owner and review schedule.
- High-impact changes should trigger an update and review of affected user plans.
- Automated answers should cite the underlying pathway record rather than generate unsupported requirements.
- Country coverage, assessment availability, and application support should be separate statuses.
- Advice boundaries and escalation rules should be agreed with qualified immigration professionals.

## Migration intelligence content model

| Record | Core fields |
|---|---|
| Country | Region, currency, languages, cost context, work rights, dependants, settlement, and authorities |
| Pathway | Purpose, applicant type, requirements, disqualifiers, process, timing, fees, rights, renewal, and settlement |
| Requirement | Rule, threshold, evidence, exceptions, effective date, and source |
| Profile factor | Age, education, occupation, experience, language, finances, family, and preferences |
| Match explanation | Supporting factors, gaps, unknowns, conflicts, confidence, and required review |
| Opportunity | Provider, country, pathway relevance, eligibility, deadline, verification, and destination |
| Task | Trigger, owner, due logic, instructions, evidence, and completion status |
| Specialist | Identity, credentials, jurisdiction, scope, status, languages, outcomes, and availability |
| Source | Authority, URL, publication date, review date, reviewer, and archived version |

Search should understand goals and constraints, not only country names. Useful queries include:

- Routes without a job offer
- Programmes for a given occupation
- Options within a specified budget
- Pathways that permit dependants
- Study routes with post-study work rights

Filters must be grounded in structured records rather than generated summaries alone.

## Commercial model and conversion

The website should make the boundary between free value and paid professional work explicit.

| Stage | Free value | Paid value |
|---|---|---|
| Discover | Profile assessment, pathway shortlist, explanations, and general roadmap | Optional professional verification |
| Navigate | Saved research and preparation guidance | One-to-one assessment, profile review, recommendation, and written plan |
| Apply | General checklist and education | Document review, application management, submission support, and milestones |
| Relocate | General settlement guidance | Provider coordination, pre-departure, and destination-specific assistance |

### Conversion principles

- Ask for registration after demonstrating value.
- Carry assessment context into every human handoff.
- Use service-specific calls to action instead of one generic WhatsApp message.
- Publish pricing structure, scope, and exclusions before sales conversations where possible.
- Treat a no-fit result as useful and offer preparation or alternatives.
- Measure progression and trust, not only leads and WhatsApp clicks.

## Phased roadmap

| Phase | Objective | Deliverables |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | Align promise with operational reality | Brand architecture, supported-route inventory, claims audit, data model, trust standards, and content ownership |
| Website launch | Turn the site into a useful discovery product | New homepage, pathway pages, assessment, explainable results, comparison, trust centre, pricing, and contextual handoff |
| Journey workspace | Create a persistent user relationship | Accounts after value, saved matches, roadmap, document checklist, specialist review, payments, and timeline |
| Network platform | Scale verified execution and opportunities | Specialist profiles, matching, partner workflow, verified opportunities, quality controls, and settlement providers |
| Intelligence layer | Improve decisions using outcome data | Change alerts, cohort insights, recommendation evaluation, operations analytics, and governed automation |

### Recommended launch scope

Choose a narrow set of high-demand pathways that Migra can actively support and maintain. Build the assessment, result, and pathway-page system deeply for that set. Additional countries may appear as research coverage with clear status labels.

### Operational work required before design is final

- Confirm supported countries, routes, and professional jurisdictions.
- Define which decisions the system can make and which require specialist review.
- Document the current WhatsApp, sales, assessment, and case-management workflow.
- Specify partner verification and ongoing quality processes.
- Audit public metrics and security statements for evidence.
- Agree the initial pathway schema and update responsibility.

## Measurement framework

| Objective | Leading measures | Outcome measures |
|---|---|---|
| Deliver clarity | Assessment completion, comprehension, and comparison use | Users who identify a realistic next step |
| Build trust | Source opens, trust-centre engagement, and specialist-profile views | Confidence, complaint, and referral rates |
| Improve qualification | Match feedback and review requests by pathway | Professional reviews confirming the system direction |
| Increase readiness | Roadmap activation and checklist completion | Applicants reaching submission with fewer preventable gaps |
| Convert responsibly | Qualified review requests and pricing views | Paid conversion, completion, and refund rate |
| Improve outcomes | Milestone completion, document rework, and response time | Decisions, time to outcome, and user-defined success |
| Protect quality | Content freshness, verification renewals, and flagged listings | Correction time, fraud incidents, and partner quality |

### North star direction

A useful long-term north star is the number of people who reach a verified next milestone on a realistic migration pathway.

This is better than traffic, registrations, or WhatsApp clicks because it connects product value with user progress without equating activity with visa approval.

## Decisions to make after the founder conversation

| Decision | Why it changes the website |
|---|---|
| Is Migra primarily a technology product, managed service, or marketplace in the next 12 months? | Determines what the homepage demonstrates and which journeys receive product depth. |
| Which segment and pathway create the strongest demand and outcomes? | Determines launch focus, assessment questions, and proof. |
| What parts of the current journey are operational? | Separates credible launch features from longer-term vision. |
| What proprietary data, workflow, or partner advantage exists? | Reveals the defensible product story. |
| Who provides regulated advice and in which jurisdictions? | Controls language, escalation, verification, and risk. |
| How are recommendations created and reviewed today? | Defines the first matching system and content governance. |
| What constitutes a successful customer outcome? | Shapes claims, metrics, stories, and the north star. |
| What is the current revenue engine and operational constraint? | Prevents demand the team cannot serve. |
| What should remain on WhatsApp and what should move into the product? | Defines channel roles and handoff architecture. |
| How ambitious should the first release be? | Sets the boundary between launch, workspace, and marketplace phases. |

### Creative direction inputs to request

- Brands the founder admires and what specifically feels right about them
- Whether Migra should feel more institutional, technological, human, or aspirational
- The emotional state users should leave the homepage with
- Visual elements that must remain from the current identity
- Real customer, specialist, product, and operational assets available for the design

## Recommended next workshop

Use the founder conversation to produce a one-page strategic brief before reviewing design templates. The brief should lock:

- Primary audience
- First product promise
- Launch pathways
- Commercial model
- Trust proof
- Channel roles
- Scope boundary

Design inspiration can then be judged against strategy rather than taste alone.

| Session | Output |
|---|---|
| Founder intent and reality | Vision, current operating model, constraints, and strongest evidence |
| Product focus | Primary user, first wedge, launch pathways, and service boundary |
| Experience architecture | Sitemap, homepage narrative, assessment journey, and conversion model |

| Delivery planning | Release scope, owners, data requirements, dependencies, and measures |

## Working recommendation

Do not begin with a cosmetic redesign of the current pages.

Begin with the assessment result and pathway-detail experience because these screens embody Migra's product promise. Once they are credible, design the homepage to reveal that value and guide people into it.

The immediate next input is the founder conversation. Review it against the decisions above, then translate the result into a creative brief and prioritized website specification.
